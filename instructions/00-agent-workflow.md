\# InfinityCrawler Coding-Agent Workflow

\#\# Mandatory Progress Timestamps

After completing each distinct implementation, testing, validation, review, or reporting step, print a timestamp immediately after the completion/update message for that step.

Requirements:

\- Timezone: South Africa Standard Time (SAST, UTC+02:00).  
\- Format: \`YYYY-MM-DD HH:mm SAST\`.  
\- Timestamp every completed step as work progresses, not only the final completion message.  
\- The final completion message must also include a SAST timestamp.  
\- Do not batch several completed steps under one later timestamp if they were reported separately.

\#\# Applicability

This workflow applies to all coding-agent tasks for the InfinityCrawler project unless Sean explicitly overrides it in the current instruction.
